Privacy Policy

How website information should be handled.

Information we collect

When the website is connected to a working enquiry system, information submitted through its forms may include your name, contact details, requested service and message.

How information may be used

Enquiry details should be used only to respond to requests, provide professional services, maintain records where required by law and protect the website.

Data security and retention

Appropriate access controls, secure storage and a documented retention policy should be implemented before collecting real client information. Do not submit sensitive financial or identity documents through the current demonstration forms.

Third-party services

Hosting, analytics, email or form providers may process information if enabled. Their details should be added here before the site is launched.

Contact

For privacy-related queries, use the contact page. Official contact details must be added by KMMC before launch.

This is a starter policy template, not legal advice. Review it for the actual data practices and applicable law before publishing.
